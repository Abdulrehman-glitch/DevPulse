# Code of conduct

DevPulse expects respectful, professional collaboration. Participants must engage in good faith, critique work rather than people, respect privacy and security boundaries, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, or publication of another person’s private information.

Maintainers may edit, reject, hide, lock, or remove contributions and restrict participation when conduct threatens safety, inclusion, project integrity, or productive collaboration. Enforcement should be proportionate, documented privately when sensitive, and mindful of both impact and context.

During private staging, authorized participants should raise conduct concerns directly through the existing private repository channel. A public reporting route will be documented when community participation opens. Do not place sensitive personal or security information in a public issue.
